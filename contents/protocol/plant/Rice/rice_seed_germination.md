# Rice seed germination

<div style='border-left: 4px solid #f0ad4e; padding: 6px 16px; margin: 8px 0;'>All ddH<sub>2</sub>O used for this process should be sterilized.</div>

## **Step 1: Homogenization**

1. Dehusked the seeds. Select only seeds that are well-developed, fully-filled, clean, and transparent. 

2. Dry-incubate the seeds in dark under 45&deg;C for 24 hours.

## **Step 2: Sterilization**

1. Soak the seeds in 3% bleach solution ( add one drop of detergent ) and gently inverting / shaking for 20 mins.

2. Wash at least 6 times with ddH<sub>2</sub>O, until the bubble has gone and water is clear.

## **Step 3: Priming and imbibition**

1. Remove the water thoroughly with pipette.

2. (optional) Seed priming. Soak the seeds in 3% KNO<sub>3</sub> solution under room temperature for 24 hours;</i>

> - The KNO<sub>3</sub> priming method is not effective in our case

3. Soak the seeds in 1 ~ 2% Plant Preservative Mixture&trade; ( PPM&trade; ) solution and incubate at 30&deg;C in dark for 16 ~ 24 hours.

> - For example, add 300 <i>&micro;</i>L PPM into 30 mL sterilized ddH<sub>2</sub>O.

## **Step 4: Sowing**

1. Remove the PPM solution.

2. Sow the seeds on suitable medium.

> - The seed must be fully embedded into the medium, otherwise, the seedling will push itself up during root penetrating into the gel.
> - Optional: Add 0.05 ~ 0.2% ( v / v ) PPM&trade; to the medium, if it is difficult to control contamination. The PPM&trade; can be autoclaved together with the media.
