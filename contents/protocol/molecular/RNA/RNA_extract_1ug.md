<div style='font-size: 1em; text-align: justify;'>
  <sup>&alpha;</sup> <b>Buffer RLT mixture</b>: Add 10 <i>&mu;</i>L <i>&beta;</i>-mercaptoethanol ( <i>&beta;</i>-ME ) to 1 mL Buffer RLT. The mixture can be stored at room temperature for up to 1 month. Buffer RLT contains a high concentration of guanidine isothiocycanate, which supports the binding of RNA to the silica membrane. The <i>&beta;</i>-mercaptoethanol should be added to Buffer RLT to inactivate RNases.
  <br>
  <sup>&beta;</sup> <b>Buffer RW1</b> contains a guanidine salt, as well as ethanol, and is used as a stringent washing buffer that efficiently removes biomolecules such as carbohydrates, proteins, fatty acids etc., that are non-specifically bound to the silica membrane. At the same time, RNA molecules larger than 200 bases remain bound to the column.
  <br>
  <sup>&gamma;</sup> <b>Buffer RPE working solution</b>: Add 4 volumes of &geq; 96% ethanol to Buffer RPE. In this case, add 2000 &mu;L ethanol to 500 &mu;L Buffer RPE. It is a mild washing buffer for removing traces of salts, which are still on the column due to buffers used earlier in the protocol.
  <br>
  <sup>&delta;</sup> <b>DNase &#8544; incubation mix</b>: Add <b style='color: olive'>10 &mu;L DNase &#8544; stock solution</b> to <b style='color: olive'>70 &mu;L Buffer RDD</b>, mix by inverting the tube, and put on ice.
</div>

> - <b>DNase &#8544; stock solution</b>: Located at 4&deg;C (b). Dissolve the lyophilized DNase &#8544; in 550 &mu;L RNase-free water. To avoid loss of DNase &#8544;, do not open the vial. Inject RNase-free water into the vial using an RNase-free needle and syringe. Mix gently by inverting the vial. Do not vortex. Store the stock solution as single use aliquots at &minus;20&deg;C (9 months) or 4&deg;C (1 month). Do not refreeze after thawing.
> - <b>Buffer RDD</b>: Located at 4&deg;C (b). It provides efficient on-column digestion of DNA and also ensures that the RNA remains bound to the column.

# Procedure

<div style='border-left: 5px solid #cc3300; text-align: justify'>
  <ul>
    <li>Ensure that all instruments are properly sterilized before starting the experiment to prevent RNA contamination and eliminate residual RNase activity.</li>
    <li>Perform all procedures prior to the elution step in a fume hood due to the hazardous nature of <i>&beta;</i>-mercaptoethanol (<i>&beta;</i>-ME).</li>
  </ul>
</div>

## **Step 1: Cell lysis**

1. Dissect &leq; 5 mg tissue ( around 30 root tips ) and dab the surface dry gently (better to use KimWipes paper). 

> - Put the tissue into an eppendorf, slightly centrifuge to spin down the tissue to the bottom of the eppendorf. If the tissue is not used immediately, store the sample at -80&deg;C.

3. Close the eppendorf lid tightly, and then immediately put the eppendorf into liquid nitrogen ( it will float up by itself, no worry ).

4. The drill of the TissueLyser instrument should be pre-cold, by inserting it into the liquid nitrogen.

5. Take out the eppendorf and powderize the tissue with the TissueLyser instrument ( this step is extremely important to yield high quantity and quality of RNA, and this step also should be finished as quick as possible ).

6. Add <b style='color: darkorange'>350 &mu;L Buffer RLT mixture</b> into the eppendorf and immediately vortex to homogenize the buffer and the powderized tissue ( 10-15 secs )

7. Now we get the <b style='color: darkorange'>raw lysate</b>. Place the eppendorf on ice to inhibit enzymatic activity of the lysate.

## **Step 2: Collect the lysate supernatant**

1. Centrifuge the raw lysate ( 3 mins, 15000 rpm ).

2. Carefully take the supernatant to a new eppendorf, and then place on ice.

3. Add <b style='color: darkorange'>350 &mu;L 70% EtOH</b> into the sample, mix well with pipetting. <i><b style='color: red'>DO NOT centrifuge</b></i>. Proceed to the next step immediately.

## **Step 3: Wash (1)**

1. Transfer the sample ( including any precipitate that may have formed ) to an <b style='color: darkorange'>RNeasy MinElute&reg; spin column</b> in a <b style='color: orange'>2 mL collection tube</b>. Close the lid and centrifuge ( <b style='color: darkorange'>4&deg;C, 1 min, &geq; 15000 rpm</b> ). <b style='color: purple'>Discard the flow-through and reuse the collection tube</b> for the next step.

2. Add <b style='color: darkorange'>350 &mu;L Buffer RW1</b> to the RNeasy MinElute&reg; spin column. Close the lid and centrifuge ( <b style='color: darkorange'>4&deg;C, 1 min, &geq; 15000 rpm</b> ). <b style='color: purple'>Discard the flow-through and reuse the collection tube</b> for the next step.

<div style='page-break-after: always;'></div>

## **Step 4: Digest DNA**

1. Add the <b style='color: darkorange'>DNase I incubation mix (80 &mu;L)</b> to the RNeasy MinElute&reg; spin column membrane. Place on benchtop at room temperature for <b style='color: darkorange'>15 mins ~ 1 hour</b>.

> -  Be sure to add the DNase I incubation mix directly to the RNeasy MinElute spin column membrane. DNase digestion will be incomplete if part of the mix sticks to the walls or the O-ring of the spin column

> - The <b>DNase I incubation mix (80 &mu;L)</b> should be fresh-prepared. Add 10 µL DNase I stock solution to 70 µL Buffer RDD, mix by inverting the tube, and put on ice. Please refer to the first page for details.

## **Step 5: Wash (2)**

1. Add <b style='color: darkorange'>350 &mu;L Buffer RW1</b> to the RNeasy MinElute&reg; spin column. Close the lid and centrifuge ( <b style='color: darkorange'>4&deg;C, 1 min, &geq; 15000 rpm</b> ). <b style='color: purple'>Discard the flow-through and collection tube</b>.

2. Place the RNeasy MinElute&reg; spin column in a <b style='color: darkorange'>new 2 mL collection tube</b> (supplied). Add <b style='color: darkorange'>500 &mu;L Buffer RPE</b> to the spin column. Close the lid and centrifuge ( <b style='color: darkorange'>4&deg;C, 1 min, &geq; 15000 rpm</b> ). <b style='color: purple'>Discard the flow-through and reuse the collection tube</b>.

3. Add <b style='color: darkorange'>500 &mu;L 80% EtOH</b> to the spin column. Close the lid and centrifuge 2 times ( <b style='color: darkorange'>2 &times; 2 mins, &geq; 15000 rpm</b> ). <b style='color: purple'>Discard the flow-through and collection tube</b>.

> -  After centrifugation, carefully remove the RNeasy MinElute spin column from the collection tube so that the column does not contact the flow-through. Otherwise, carryover of ethanol will occur.

4. Place the RNeasy MinElute&reg; spin column in a <b style='color: darkorange'>new 2 mL collection tube</b> (supplied). <b style='color: red'>Open the lid</b> and centrifuge at full speed ( <b style='color: darkorange'>4&deg;C, 15 mins, &geq; 15000 rpm</b> ) to dry the membrane. <b style='color: purple'>Discard the flow-through and collection tube</b>.

> - To avoid damage to their lids, place the spin columns into the centrifuge with at least one empty position between columns. Orient the lids so that they point in a direction opposite to the rotation of the rotor (e.g., if the rotor rotates clockwise, orient the lids counterclockwise)

## **Step 6: Elute RNA**

1. Place the RNeasy MinElute&reg; spin column in a <b style='color: orange'>new 1.5 mL RNase-free low-binding collection tube</b> (supplied). 

2. Add <b style='color: darkorange'>14 <i>&micro;</i>L RNase-free water</b> directly to the center of the spin column membrane. Close the lid gently.

3. Centrifuge at full speed ( <b style='color: darkorange'>4&deg;C, 1 ~ <u>15 mins</u>, &geq; 15000 rpm</b> ) to elute the RNA.

4. Store the RNA eluate at <b>&minus;20&deg;C</b> ( few weeks ) or <b>&minus;80&deg;C</b> ( > 1 year ).

# **RNA quantification with Qubit&trade;**

### <a href='https://www.thermofisher.com/order/catalog/product/Q32852'>Qubit&trade; RNA HS Assay Kit</a>

# Procedure

## Materials

> For best results, ensure that all materials and reagents are at room temperature.

1. Set up the required number of Qubit&trade; Assay Tubes for standards and samples. This assay requires 2 standards ( Component C and D ).
- <i>**Note**: Use only thin-wall, clear, 0.5 mL PCR tubes ( Cat. No. <a href='https://www.thermofisher.com/search/results?query=Q32856'>Q32856</a> ) for the Qubit&trade; 4 Fluorometer.</i>

2. Label the tube lids.
- <i>**Note**: Do not label the side of the tubes, that will affect light penetration. Label the lid of each standard tube correctly, as the calibration of the Qubit&trade; Fluorometer requires the standards to be inserted into the instrument in the right order.</i>

## Step 1: Make working solution
The working solution is a mixture of Component A (dye) and Component B (HS buffer), with a ratio of 1 : 199. For example, to make 10 tubes (8 samples + 2 standards), add 10 &mu;L dye into 1990 &mu;L HS buffer.

1. Prepare the <b style='color: orange'>working solution</b> by diluting Component A 1:200 in Component B for each tube ( sample + standard solutions ). For example, 10 &mu;L Component A + 1990 &mu;L Component B for 10 tubes ( 8 samples + 2 standards ).
- <i>**Note**: <b style='color: red'>DO NOT</b> mix the working solution in a glass container. Use clean plastic tube</i>

## Step 2: Prepare standard solutions


<br>
<div style='page-break-after: always;'></div>

## Step 3: Prepare sample solutions

2. Add the working solution to each tube such that the final volume is 200 &mu;L.

> |                                  | Standard assay tubes  | User sample assay tubes  |
> | :-----:                          | :-----:               | :-----:                  |
> | Volume of working solution       | 190 &mu;L          | 180 &ndash; 199 &mu;L |
> | Volume of standard               | 10 &mu;L           | &mdash;                  |
> | Volume of user sample            | &mdash;               | 1 &ndash; 20 &mu;L    |
> | Total volume in each assay tube  | 200 &mu;L          | 200 &mu;L             |
> ||||

5. Add <b style='color: orange'>10 &mu;L</b> of Component C ( <b style='color: orange'>standard # 1</b> ) and D ( <b style='color: orange'>standard # 2</b> ) separately to the appropriate tubes.
6. Add <b style='color: orange'>1 &mu;L</b> of each user <b style='color: orange'>sample</b> to the appropriate tubes.
7. Vigorously vortex for 3 ~ 5 seconds.
8. Incubate at room temperature for 2 minutes. Then proceed to read standards and samples with the Qubit&trade; 4 Fluorometer.

## Reading standards and samples

1. On the **`Home`** screen, touch **`RNA`**, then select **`RNA High Sensitivity`** as the assay type. Touch **`Read standards`** to proceed.
2. Insert the tube containing **Standard # 1** into the sample chamber, close the lid, then touch **`Read standard`**. When the reading is comnplete, remove Standard # 1.
3. Insert the tube containing **Standard # 2** into the sample chamber, close the lid, then touch **`Read standard`**. When the reading is comnplete, remove Standard # 2.
4. Touch **`Run samples`**.
5. On the assay screen, select the **`Sample volume`** and **`Unit`**.
- Touch the + or - buttons on the wheel, or anywhere on the wheel itself, to select the sample volume added to the assay tube ( 1 &mu;L in this protocol ).
- From the **`Unit`** dropdown menu, select the units for the output sample concentration.
6. Insert a sample tube into the sample chamber, close the lid, then touch **`Read tube`**. When the reading is complete ( ~ 3 seconds ), remove the sample tube.
- The top value ( in large font ) is the concentration of the original sample and the bottom value is the dilution concentration.
7. Repeat step 6 until all samples have been read.
