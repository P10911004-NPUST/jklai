Procedure

All instruments should be sterilized prior to the experiment

Step 1: Gel-Dye Mix

One set of Gel-Dye Mix is for one chip ( 12 wells ).

  1. Allow the RNA dye concentrate ( 🔵 ) equilibrate to room temperature ( 25°C ) for 30 mins.

  2. Vortex the RNA dye concentrate ( 🔵 ) for 10 secs, spin down and add 1 μL dye into a 65 μL gel matrix aliquot.

  3. Vortex solution well. Centrifuge ( 13,000 g, 10 mins ) at 25°C. Use the prepared Gel-Dye Mix within one day.

Step 2: Decontaminate the instrument electrode

  1. Pipette 350 μL RNaseZap™ solution into an Electrode Cleaner ( we reuse this, marked as "RNaseZap" ). Close the machine lid to submerge the electrode probe into the solution for 3 mins.

  2. Pipette 350 μL RNase-free water into an Electrode Cleaner ( we reuse this, marked as "RNase-free water" ). Close the machine lid to submerge the electrode probe into the water for 3 mins.

  3. Keep the lid opened for 1 min to air-dry the electrode.

During this period, switch on PC and execute the 2100 Expert software

Step 3: Load Gel-Dye Mix

  1. Put a new RNA Nano Chip on the Chip Priming Station.

  2. Pipette 9 μL of Gel-Dye Mix into the well marked G ( only one well ).

  3. Make sure the plunger is positioned at 1 mL and then close the Chip Priming Station. Make sure you hear a click sound to confirm that the latch is securely snapped.

  4. Press the plunger until it is held by the clip.

  5. Wait for exactly 30 secs then release clip.

  6. Wait for 5 secs. Slowly pull back plunger to 1 mL position. Open the Chip Priming Station.

  7. Pipette 9 μL of Gel-Dye Mix into the wells marked G ( two wells ).

  8. Discard the remaining Gel-Dye Mix.

Step 4: Load Marker

  1. Pipette 5 μL of RNA marker in all 12 sample wells.

  2. Pipette 5 μL of RNA marker in the ladder well marked with a ladder symbol 🪜 ( only one well ).

  • We prepared 75 μL marker per vial. So now we already load in 65 μL into the chip. The remaining 10 μL is, for the empty sample wells, in case you don't have 12 RNA samples. The machine requires all sample wells must have same volume.

Step 5: Load Ladder

  1. Pipette 1 μL of RNA ladder in the ladder well marked with a ladder symbol 🪜 ( only one well ).

Step 6: Load samples RNA

  1. Pipette 1 μL of sample in each of the 12 sample wells.
  • substitute with 1 μL Marker for unused sample wells.

  1. Put the chip horizontally in the IKA vortexer and vortex for 1 min at 2400 rpm. Hover your hand over the chip because it might fly out due to an overly strong vortex.

  2. Run the chip in the Agilent 2100 Bioanalyzer instrument within 5 mins.

Step 7: On-machine

  1. Put the chip into the slot ( the shape of the upper right corner of the chip should be matched ). Close the machine lid gently to submerge the electrode into the chip.

  2. Execute the 2100 Expert software.

  3. Navigate to Assays → RNA → Plant RNA Nano.xsy.

  4. Key in your sample name / ID to the list at lower left side.

  5. Make sure the Start Run Checklist at the lower right side are all checked.

  6. Press Start and wait for 5 ~ 10 mins for the machine checking.

  7. Wait for ≈ 40 mins for the chip to be read.

  8. After finishing, discard the chip and clean up the electrode as the procedure in Step 2: Decontaminate the instrument electrode. You can skip the RNaseZap step.

  9. Check and save all the data with a single PDF report, use File → Print
