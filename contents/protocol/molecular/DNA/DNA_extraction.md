# **DNA extraction**

Buffer solutions usage per sample:

| Material                       | Quantity per sample | Note                           |
| :----------                    | :---:               | :---                           |
| DNA extraction buffer          | 400 &micro;L        | See the last page              |
| Isopropanol (2-propanol)       | 300 &micro;L        | Around 70% of the supernatant  |
| Ethanol (EtOH)                 | 1000 &micro;L       | DNase-free ethanol             |
| Tris-EDTA                      | 100 &micro;L        | Commercial product             |
||||

# Procedure

## **Step 1: Cell lysis**

> Caution: If directly powderized the sample with liquid nitrogen within the ependorf, after filling in the liquid nitrogen into the eppendorf, <b style='color: red'>DO NOT</b> close the lid, because the eppendorf will explode when temperature raise.

1. Put two sterilized steel balls into each 1.5 mL eppendorf.

2. Add <b style='color:darkorange'>400 <i>&micro;</i>L DNA extraction buffer</b> into each eppendorf.

3. Directly submerge the samples into the DNA extraction buffer in each eppendorf.

4. Disrupt the tissue using Tissue Grinder ( <b style='color:darkorange'>30 hits / sec</b>, <b style='color:darkorange'>10 mins</b> ).

> - Place a thick layer of tissue paper between the tissue grinder adapter cover and the eppendorf tube caps to boost grinding efficiency and prevent the tube caps from popping open during high-speed reciprocal shaking.

6. Centrifuge at room temperature ( <b style='color: darkorange'>&ge;15000 rpm</b>, <b style='color: darkorange'>20 mins</b> ) to bring down the bubbles;

7. Dry bath ( <b style='color: darkorange'>60&deg;C</b>, <b style='color: darkorange'>1 hour</b> ).

## **Step 2: Disrupt protein&mdash;DNA interactions**

1. Add <b style='color:darkorange'>400 <i>&micro;</i>L 5 M NaCl</b> ( same volume with the extraction buffer, therefore the NaCl final concentration will be 2.5 M );

2. Centrifuge at room temperature ( > 15000 rpm, 20 mins ) to bring down the remaining tissues; 

<div style='page-break-after: always;'></div>

## **Step 3: Collect DNA supernatant**

1. Carefully take the supernatant ( around 90% of the supernatant volume ) to a new 1.5 mL eppendorf;

## **Step 4: Precipitate DNA**

1. Add <b style='color:darkorange'>300 <i>&micro;</i>L isopropanol</b> ( around 70% of the supernatant volume ) into the supernatant, leave overnight at -20&deg;C;

2. Centrifuge at 4&deg;C ( > 15000 rpm, 20 mins ) to bring down the DNA;

3. Discard the supernatant carefully. Do not touch the tube bottom, which sticking with the DNA palette;

## **Step 5: Wash**

1. Add <b style='color:darkorange'>200 <i>&micro;</i>L 70% ethanol</b> and gently invert the tube;

2. Centrifuge at 4&deg;C ( > 15000 rpm, 20 mins ) to bring down the DNA;

3. Discard the ethanol carefully with small pipette;

## **Step 6: Elute DNA**

1. Dry bath at 60&deg;C for around 10 mins, the ethanol should be totally gone;

2. Add <b style='color:darkorange'>50 <i>&micro;</i>L DNase-free water or TE buffer ( pH 8.0 )</b> and dry bath at 60&deg;C for around 10 mins to dissolve the DNA palette;

3. After the DNA was fully dissolved, slightly centrifuge at room temperature to bring down all solution on the tube wall;

> - Store the purified DNA at &minus;20&deg;C for up to 2 years. Avoid repeated freeze–thaw cycles and vigorous mixing.

# DNA quality assurance

1. Check the DNA concentration and quality.

- Reference: https://ntuhmc.ntuh.gov.tw/epaper-57th.htm

2. Standardize the DNA concentration according to the lowest concentration of the samples.

- At least 10 ng / <i>&micro;</i>L for PCR
- the diluted DNA should be used as soon as possible

### **Wavelength detection**

| Wavelength (nm) | Detected Compound                                                      |
| :---:           | :---                                                                   |
| 230             | EtOH, EDTA, carbohydrates, phenol, Guanidone HCL (for DNA isolation)   |
| 260             | RNA,ssDNA, dsDNA, guanidine isothiocyabate (for RNA isolation)         |
| 270             | Phenolic solution (TRIzol for RNA isolation)                           |
| 280             | Protein, phenol, other contamination                                   |
|||

### Ideal wavelength ratio (quality indicator):
| Nucleic acid  | 260 / 280 nm  | 260 / 230 nm | 260 / 270 nm |
| :---:         | :---:         | :---:        | :---:        |
| DNA           | ~ 1.8         | > 2.0        | > 1.2        |
| RNA           | ~ 2.0         | > 2.0        | > 1.2        |
|||||

# Stock solutions

## **DNA extraction buffer**

| Chemical                                   | Concentration    | Addition |
| :----------                                | :---:            | :---:    |
| 1 M Tris-HCl (pH 7.5) <sup>&#8251;</sup>   | 200 mM           | 20 mL    |
| 5 M NaCl                                   | 250 mM           | 5 mL     |
| 500 mM EDTA <sup>&dagger;</sup>            | 25 mM            | 5 mL     |
| 10% SDS <sup>&sect;</sup>                  | 0.5% (v/v)       | 5 mL     |
| ddH<sub>2</sub>O                           |                  | 65 mL    |
| Total:                                     |                  | 100 mL   |
||||

## **<sup>&#8251;</sup> Tris-HCl stock solution ( 1 M )**

1. Dissolve 121.1 g of Tris base in 800 mL of ddH<sub>2</sub>O.

2. Adjust the pH to the desired value by adding concentrated HCl.

3. Allow the solution to cool to room temperature before making final adjustments to the pH.

4. Adjust the volume of the solution to 1 L with ddH<sub>2</sub>O. Dispense into aliquots and sterilize by autoclaving.

> | pH    | 12N HCl addition |
> | :---: | :---:            |
> | 7.4   | 70 mL            |
> | 7.6   | 60 mL            |
> | 8.0   | 42 mL            |
> |||

## **<sup>&dagger;</sup>  EDTA stock solution ( 0.5 M, pH 8.0 )**

> EDTA ( Ethylenediamenetetraacetic acid )  

1. Add 186.1 g of disodium EDTA &bullet; 2H<sub>2</sub>O to 800 mL of ddH<sub>2</sub>O.

2. Stir vigorously on a magnetic stirrer to dissolve the EDTA.

3. Adjust the pH to 8.0 with NaOH ( ~ 20 g of NaOH pellets ), otherwise the disodium salt of EDTA will not dissolve.

4. Dispense into aliquots and sterilize by autoclaving.

<div style='page-break-after: always;'></div>

## **<sup>&sect;</sup>  SDS stock solution ( 10% )**

> Sodium dodecyl sulfate, sodium lauryl sulfate  
1. Dissolve 100 g of electropheresis-grade SDS in 900 mL of ddH<sub>2</sub>O.

2. Heat to 68&deg;C and stir with a magnetic stirrer to assist dissolution. If necessary, adjust the pH to 7.2 by adding a few drops of concentrated HCl.

3. Adjust the volume to 1 L with ddH<sub>2</sub>O.

4. Store at room temperature. Sterilization is not necessary. <b style='color:red'>DO NOT</b> autoclave.
