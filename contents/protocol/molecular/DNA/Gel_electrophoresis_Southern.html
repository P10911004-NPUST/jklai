Gel electrophoresis (Southern)

Usage amount per slot:

Material Concentration Final concentration Location
PCR product -- -- 4°C
TAE buffer ※ 50X 0.5X II
Agarose gel † -- 3% II
SYBR® stock solution * 1000X 1X II
Loading Dye 6X 1X 4°C (c)
RTU-100 (Ladder marker) -- -- 4°C (c)

※ To prepare the 0.5X TAE buffer, make at least 1 L, as each run requires at least 500 mL 0.5X TAE buffer.

† The big gel slot requires around 40 mL gel solution; the small gel slot requires 20 mL gel solution. For example, to make a 3% big gel, add 1.2 g agarose powder (ultra pure grade) to 40 mL 0.5X TAE buffer.

* To prepare 1000X SYBR stock solution, dilutes 100 μL SYBR® raw solution with 900 μL DMSO. This chemical is used to stain the DNA and detected by UV radiation.

Procedure

Step 1: Make gel

  1. Prepare the gel. After adding agarose powder into the 0.5X TAE buffer, melt them with microwave. Gently shake the vial during each time points.
  • use mid-low firepower, 1 mins → 30 secs → add ddH2O → 20 secs → 10 secs
  1. Add 0.001 volume of SYBR® stock solution into the gel solution immediately ( 1000X → 1X ), gently shake to mix them well;
  • For example, 40 mL gel requires 40 μL 1000X SYBR stock solution.
  1. Pour the gel into the gel container and wait for solidification (around 15 mins);

Step 2: Set up the electrode machine

  1. Prepare the electropheresis machine, the electrode direction should be from negative to positive;

  2. After the gel was solidified, put the gel container together with the gel into the elctrophoresis machine;

  3. Add some TAE buffer (same strength with the gel; 0.5X in this case) into the electropheresis machine and make sure the gel was fully submerged in the buffer;

Step 3: Load sample

  1. Add loading dye to the PCR product. The loading dye final concentration should be 1X. For example, 2 μL 6X loading dye + 10 μL PCR product;

  2. Gently inject 2 μL ladder marker into the first and the last wells of the gel;

  3. Gently inject the blank and the PCR products ( around 6 μL ) into the wells;

Step 4: Start running

  1. Set the voltage as 135 V and the runtime as 40 mins;
  • 5 ~ 10 V / cm for DNA size < 1 kb;
  • 4 ~ 10 V / cm for DNA size between 1 ~ 12 kb;
  • 1 ~ 3 V / cm for DNA size greater than 12 kb.
    For our machine, the distance between the electrodes is around 13.5 cm.

Step 5: Gel-imaging

  1. Turn on the machine ( Bio-Rad Gel Doc XR+ ).

  2. Put in the gel.

  3. Start up the Image Lab software.

  4. New protocol → Gel imaging.

  5. Application → Select → Nucleic acid → SYBR® Safe.

  6. Position Gel → Filter 1 → adjust gel position.

  7. Run protocol.

  8. Export for publication ( Export for analysis is 16-bit images ).


Done !!!
